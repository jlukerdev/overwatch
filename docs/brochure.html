OverWatch

Views

Lay it out your way

Build as many named Views as you like. Give the cameras that matter more room, and switch Views with the 1–9 keys.

Fill and zoom

Look closer

Click any camera to fill the window, then zoom in. Hold one button to unlock the door that camera watches.

Doors · with UniFi Access

Every lock at a glance

See which doors are locked, lock or unlock any of them, and start a lockdown or evacuation from one screen.

RTSP streams · no console needed

Any camera with an RTSP address

No UniFi console? Switch to RTSP streams mode, add each camera's address, and watch them in the same Views, with the same fill, zoom and audio.

Rotating cells

One cell cycles through several cameras, or jumps to the one that sees motion.

Quality per camera

High where you need detail, low where you don't.

Audio

Listen to one camera at a time.

Focus mode

Hide everything but the video.

Stays on your network

No cloud.No account.

OverWatch talks only to your own UniFi console, using Ubiquiti's official API. In RTSP streams mode it talks only to your cameras. Your API key and stream addresses are encrypted by your computer's keystore.

Mac

macOS 12 (Monterey) or later.

Windows

Windows 10 or 11.

You'll need

A UniFi Protect console on your network or VPN, or cameras with an RTSP address. UniFi Access for doors.

OverWatch is a personal project, not a Ubiquiti product. The film shows the running app with illustrated camera pictures; the other screens are illustrations.
